Delete your Good Parent account

The Good Company · Good Parent

An account is optional. If you never signed in, there is no account and nothing of yours is on our servers.

Deleting your account removes the copy on our servers. It does not touch the records on your phone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Good Parent and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Your account.
  3. Tap Delete account and server copy, then confirm.

This works the same on Android and iPhone.

Without the app

If you have already uninstalled the app, email remixglitch@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Good Parent account". We will delete the account and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your account, the email address attached to it, and every record synced to our servers: custody events, journal notes, expenses and receipts, calendar events, contacts, health entries and growth measurements.

Deletion is prompt — a matter of days, not weeks — including from routine backups as they rotate. Nothing is kept afterwards.

What is not deleted

Records on your phone stay on your phone. To remove those too, use Settings → Delete everything on this device, or uninstall the app.

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or the App Store, not by us. Deleting your account does not cancel one. Cancel it in the Play Store (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings.
