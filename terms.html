Records, exports, and what a court will make of them

Good Parent links your entries in a sequence using cryptographic hashes. That is designed to show ORDER and NON-ALTERATION: if an earlier entry is changed afterwards, the link visibly breaks.

That is all it shows. It does not prove when an entry was made — the clock belongs to your device, which you control. It does not prove that the events you described actually happened. It does not make anything admissible.

Optional independent timestamping (RFC 3161) adds an outside authority’s attestation that specific data existed by a given date. It still says nothing about whether what you wrote is true.

Whether any export from this app is admissible, relevant, or persuasive is entirely a matter for a court, and depends on your jurisdiction, your case, and the rules of evidence. We make no representation that any export will be accepted by any court, tribunal, mediator or agency, and we are not liable for any outcome in any proceeding.

We are not a party to your dispute with anybody. If you are in or near litigation, speak to a lawyer about what to record and how.
